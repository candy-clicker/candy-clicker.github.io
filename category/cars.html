Driving Techniques & Balance Strategies

In physics-based titles like Drive Mad, full throttle is rarely your friend. Modulate your acceleration carefully, tap the brakes in mid-air to balance your vehicle's pitch, and maintain all four wheels level upon landing. When drifting on tight corners, initiate the turn before pulling the handbrake to keep your engine RPM in the sweet spot.

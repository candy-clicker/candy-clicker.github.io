Tactical Tips for Higher Killstreaks

In tactical shooters like Time Shooter, stay completely motionless whenever assessing enemy positions—use this frozen time to plot your bullet-dodging path and line up clean headshots. For fast-paced 3D shooters, keep crosshairs at head height, strafe continuously between cover, and never reload out in the open.

🔬 Our Rigorous 5-Step Quality Assurance Process

To uphold Google’s highest quality rater guidelines, we do not simply aggregate links. Every title published on Candy Clicker undergoes our comprehensive 5-step evaluation protocol:

1

Security & Safety Inspection

We perform static and dynamic script audits to verify that no malicious code, third-party miners, or unauthorized trackers are loaded into player sessions.

2

Cross-Platform Performance & FPS Benchmark

We test each title across Google Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Microsoft Edge, as well as iOS and Android devices, verifying consistent 60 FPS without high CPU drain.

3

Gameplay Balance & Accessibility Review

Our reviewers assess the progression curves, clarity of controls, color contrast for visual accessibility, and overall replay value to ensure an enjoyable experience.

4

Ad Quality & User Respect Check

In accordance with Better Ads Standards, we strictly prohibit auto-playing loud audio, deceptive download buttons, or intrusive interstitials that break immersion.

5

Ongoing Maintenance & Compatibility Updates

Browser web APIs update frequently. Our engineering team monitors game health continuously to patch broken assets, deprecated APIs, and iframe layout issues promptly.

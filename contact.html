❓ Frequently Asked Support Questions (FAQ)

Why is a game showing a black screen or not loading?

Most modern HTML5 web games require WebGL and Hardware Acceleration enabled in your browser settings. If a game fails to load, try disabling strict third-party ad-block extensions for our domain, or try loading the page in an Incognito / Private window.

Are all games on Candy Clicker completely free?

Yes! Every single game hosted or embedded on Candy Clicker is 100% free to play. You do not need to purchase any subscription or install external executables.

How can I submit my game to be featured?

We welcome independent game developers! Simply select "Game Developer Submission" in the contact form above, include a playable URL, game thumbnail, and a brief description of your title. Our editorial review team will evaluate it within 3-5 business days.

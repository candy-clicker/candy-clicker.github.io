🎮 About Fireboy and Watergirl 1: Forest Temple

Co-op Adventure Elemental Puzzle Teamwork Two Player Game

Team up with elemental companions in Fireboy and Watergirl 1: Forest Temple online! Control Fireboy and Watergirl simultaneously or with a friend, solving intricate environmental puzzles, flipping pressure levers, and gathering glowing diamonds across ancient temples.

⭐ Key Highlights

  • Simultaneous 2-player cooperative gameplay on a single keyboard
  • Unique elemental mechanics: Fireboy rules lava, Watergirl rules water
  • Ancient temple mechanisms: light beams, mirrors, and push blocks
  • Rankings based on gem collection and completion speed

🎯 Quick Guide

  • Guide Fireboy through red lava and Watergirl through blue lakes
  • Keep both characters away from deadly green toxic ooze
  • Coordinate button presses to open gates for your partner
  • Bring both heroes to their respective exit doors to win

🎮 Controls

  • Arrow Keys: Move and jump with Fireboy
  • A, W, D: Move and jump with Watergirl
  • Collaboration: Move together to clear puzzles simultaneously
